# Consultation Fee Structure by Specialty


Consultation fees on Practo are set per specialty and range from 300 INR to 2500 INR for a first in-clinic visit. General Medicine is billed between 300 and 700 INR, Pediatrics between 400 and 900 INR, and Dermatology between 600 and 1400 INR. Orthopedics is billed between 700 and 1600 INR and Cardiology between 900 and 2500 INR, because those specialties include longer examination slots and on-site diagnostic review. The displayed fee always covers the clinician's consultation time only; lab tests, imaging, procedures and consumables are billed separately. The fee shown at the moment of booking is the fee charged, even if the clinic revises its published rate before the visit.
