# Insurance Claim Process


To claim a consultation against insurance, the patient submits the Practo invoice, the clinician's prescription and the insurance ID through the Reimbursements section within 30 days of the visit. Practo forwards a claim packet to the insurer, and the insurer decides the outcome; Practo does not approve, reject or adjudicate any claim itself. Cashless settlement is available only at partner clinics that display the Cashless badge, and it requires pre-authorisation raised at least 24 hours before the appointment. A claim that is returned for missing documents can be resubmitted once, and the 30-day clock restarts from the date the insurer returns it. Consultations booked and paid outside Practo cannot be claimed through this process.
