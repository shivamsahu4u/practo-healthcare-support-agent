# Second Opinion Process


A patient may request a second opinion from a different clinician in the same specialty at any time, and the request is raised from the completed appointment in the Practo app. The second-opinion consultation is billed as a full first-visit consultation fee for the new clinician, and the follow-up discount does not apply to it. With the patient's explicit consent, the earlier prescription and the relevant lab reports are shared with the second clinician; without that consent the new clinician starts from scratch. A second opinion is advisory, and neither clinician is obliged to adopt the other's plan; Practo itself never arbitrates between two clinical opinions. Second opinions are available as telemedicine consultations where the specialty allows a review without a physical examination.
