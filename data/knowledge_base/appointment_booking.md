# Appointment Booking Policy


Patients may book an appointment on Practo up to 30 calendar days in advance, and same-day booking stays open until 90 minutes before the chosen consultation slot. Every booking needs a verified contact number and the patient's own Practo account, because the confirmation code and the clinic address are sent to that number. A booking is only confirmed once the slot-hold expires into a confirmed state, which normally takes under two minutes; an unconfirmed hold is released automatically after ten minutes. Each patient account may hold at most three open Scheduled appointments across all specialties at one time. Walk-in patients are accommodated only where the clinic publishes spare walk-in capacity for that day.
