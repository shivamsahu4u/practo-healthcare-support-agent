# Prescription Refill Policy


A prescription refill can be requested through Practo for up to 90 days after the consultation that issued the original prescription. Refills for routine long-term medication are approved by the prescribing clinician only, and a clinician who has never seen the patient will not issue a refill. Requests are reviewed within one working day, and the patient is notified in the app when the refreshed prescription is available to download. Controlled and habit-forming medicines are excluded from refills entirely and always need a fresh consultation. A refill request does not change any dosage; any dosage change requires a new appointment with the prescribing clinician.
