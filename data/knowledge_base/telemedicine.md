# Telemedicine Eligibility


A video or audio telemedicine consultation is available for patients aged 2 years and above, provided the patient has a stable internet connection and a verified contact number on the account. Telemedicine is suitable for follow-up reviews, report interpretation, routine prescription queries and second opinions, and it is not offered for a first visit that needs a physical examination. Pediatric telemedicine for a child under 5 years requires a parent or guardian to be present on the call for the whole consultation. Telemedicine is never appropriate for a suspected emergency; such a request is redirected to the emergency-visit route instead. Each telemedicine slot is 15 minutes, and one free 5-minute extension is granted when the clinician requests it.
