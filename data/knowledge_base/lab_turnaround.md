# Lab Test Turnaround Times


Routine pathology reports such as a complete blood count or a lipid profile are published to the patient's Practo account within 24 hours of sample collection. Standard biochemistry and thyroid panels take up to 48 hours, while culture and sensitivity tests take 3 to 5 days because the sample needs an incubation period. Histopathology and specialised genetic panels are quoted individually and typically take 7 to 10 working days. Turnaround is measured from the time the sample reaches the partner laboratory, not from the time of booking, and a repeat sample restarts the clock. If a report is delayed beyond its quoted turnaround, the patient can raise a delay ticket and the laboratory must respond within one working day.
