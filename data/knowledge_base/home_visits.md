# Home Visit Eligibility


A home visit can be booked when the patient is above 65 years of age, has a documented mobility limitation, or is recovering after a procedure in the 14 days following discharge. Home visits are offered for General Medicine and post-operative Orthopedics review only, and they are restricted to serviceable pin codes within 15 kilometres of the partner clinic. The home-visit charge is the specialty's in-clinic consultation fee plus a flat 500 INR travel component, and it must be paid before the visit is dispatched. A home visit must be booked at least 12 hours in advance, and it cannot be used as a substitute for an emergency response. Cardiology, Dermatology and Pediatrics are not available as home visits and need an in-clinic or telemedicine slot instead.
