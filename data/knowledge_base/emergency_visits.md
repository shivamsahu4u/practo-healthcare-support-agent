# Emergency Visit Protocol


Practo's booking and support channels are not an emergency service and must never be used to seek urgent medical help. A patient describing a possible emergency — for example chest pain, breathlessness, uncontrolled bleeding, a suspected stroke, a seizure or a loss of consciousness — must be told immediately to call the national emergency number 112 or to go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Support agents and automated assistants do not triage, assess severity or offer any clinical advice, and they do not attempt to identify what is wrong. Emergency walk-ins at a partner clinic are billed at the clinic's published emergency rate and are settled directly with that clinic rather than through the standard consultation-fee flow. Any appointment left open because the patient went to an emergency department is cancelled without a late-cancellation charge.
