# Follow-up Visit Discount Policy


A follow-up visit with the same clinician within 14 days of the original consultation is charged at 50 percent of the first-visit consultation fee. A follow-up between 15 and 30 days is charged at 75 percent of the first-visit fee, and after 30 days the visit is billed as a fresh first consultation at the full rate. The discount applies only when the appointment is flagged as requiring a follow-up and only for the clinician who issued the original prescription. The follow-up discount cannot be combined with any promotional coupon, and only one discounted follow-up is allowed per original consultation. A follow-up that the clinic itself reschedules keeps the discount tier of the original booking date.
