# Patient Data Privacy Policy


Practo stores patient records, prescriptions and lab reports under access control, and only the treating clinician, the patient and explicitly authorised clinic staff can open them. Support agents see a masked view in which the contact number is redacted, and they can never retrieve a clinical note or a diagnosis through the support tooling. A patient may request an export or a deletion of their own record, and the request is completed within 30 days of verification. Records are never sold, and they are shared with an insurer only when the patient has submitted a claim that specifically requires them. Every read of a patient record is written to an immutable audit log that retains the actor, the action and the timestamp.
