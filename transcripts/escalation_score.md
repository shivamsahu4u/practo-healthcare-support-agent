## Formula

```
aging_normalised = min(days_since_created, 30) / 30
escalation_score = clamp(0.45 * follow_up_required + 0.55 * aging_normalised, 0, 1)
```

Older unresolved records escalate harder. The weights overlap the two bands on
purpose: a record with no follow-up flag can still reach 0.55, and a follow-up
record starts at 0.45, so the threshold sits inside `[0.45, 0.55]` where the two
signals genuinely compete.

## Why this is not a boolean OR

- records escalated on age alone, with **no** follow-up flag: `['APT-1006', 'APT-1026', 'APT-1030', 'APT-1039']`
- records with a follow-up flag that are **not** escalated because they are still fresh: `['APT-1044']`

Both lists are non-trivial outcomes that `follow_up_required OR is_old` cannot
produce. If either list were empty the score would have collapsed into a
boolean, and that is worth checking whenever the dataset design changes.
